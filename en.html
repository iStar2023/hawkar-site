Before you ask

Questions people ask us.

If yours is not here, ask us directly. It costs nothing to ask.

Can you just send money to my family?

No — and we will always say no. Moving money across borders on someone's behalf requires a licence we do not hold and do not want. We sell work performed, not money transferred. Where a task involves buying something — groceries, a gift, gold — we buy it and deliver it, with a receipt and a photograph.

Will the American doctor treat my relative?

No. They give a second opinion, and then speak doctor-to-doctor with your own physician in Kurdistan. Your local doctor writes every prescription and order, because they are the one licensed to care for the patient. Think of it as bringing an experienced colleague into the room.

Who actually does the work in Kurdistan?

People we have vetted personally, working under our name and paid by us. Not a stranger found online, and not a favour asked of a busy cousin. If someone we send ever behaves dishonestly, we want to hear immediately — everything we have depends on it.

How do I pay from Sweden or the Netherlands?

By ordinary bank card, in euros or dollars, through our registered company in the United States. Nothing is paid inside Iraq and no Iraqi bank account is involved.

If you are paid by the hospital, are you really independent?

It is a fair question, so here is the whole arrangement. You pay the hospital directly at their own price; we never handle that money. The hospital pays us a referral fee, and we require written confirmation that our fee does not raise your price. We also present two or three options with real total costs, not one recommendation. If a hospital refuses those terms, we do not work with them.

What if I am unhappy with the result?

Tell us. Where we did not deliver what we promised, we put it right or return your money. We would rather lose one payment than lose our name — in a community where everyone talks to each other, our reputation is the only asset that really matters.

Do you work with families inside Kurdistan too?

Yes. Most of our customers live abroad and are paying for family at home, but if you are in Kurdistan and want a second opinion or help arranging treatment abroad, you are welcome.

Medical consultation notice

What our medical service is, and is not.

Consultations arranged by Hawkar are second opinions and educational consultations. The reviewing physician is licensed in the United States, is not licensed to practise medicine in Iraq, and does not become the patient's treating doctor.

They do not diagnose, prescribe, or order tests. Every prescription, test, and procedure must be ordered by the patient's own licensed local doctor, who remains fully responsible for their care.

Do not stop or change any treatment because of a Hawkar consultation without speaking to your local doctor first. In an emergency, do not use this service — go to a hospital.

Privacy
